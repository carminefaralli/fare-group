The problem

Many plants lose margin
without noticing

Rarely because of the technology. Margin is lost in how feedstock is bought and how the work is organised.

Feedstock bought by the tonne

You pay for weight, not energy. A cheap feedstock can yield very little methane and end up costing far more than it seems.

Unstable digester

An unbalanced recipe slows down the biology, cuts production and increases the digestate to handle.

Field work without procedures

When every shift works its own way and responsibilities are unclear, the same mistakes keep coming back.

Numbers that arrive too late

If loads and production are reviewed at month end, losses are found when they have already been paid for.

The problem with biogas is often not technology. It is management.

Frequently asked questions

FAR.E Group at a glance

What does FAR.E Group do?

FAR.E Group is an Italian group that helps owners, funds and developers run biogas and biomethane plants. It covers feedstock and digester recipe (feedstock management), plant operations on behalf of the owners, project management and technical and contract advisory, and it develops the Methan.IQ platform.

Where does FAR.E Group operate?

Across Italy, with a regional network that keeps us close to the plants. FAR.E Group has its registered office in Isernia and offices in Florence.

Which companies make up FAR.E Group?

FAR.E Group brings together FAR.E Energy, which runs plants on behalf of their owners, and FAR.E Services, which provides technical and contract advisory, feedstock management, project management and develops Methan.IQ.

Does FAR.E Group own plants or sell technology?

No. FAR.E Group is an independent technology operator: it does not own plants and does not sell plants or equipment. FAR.E Energy runs plants on behalf of their owners.

What is feedstock management?

It is the management of the biomass that feeds the digester: choice of feedstock, supply contracts, incoming quality and recipe. FAR.E Group judges it on the cost per cubic metre of methane produced, not on the price per tonne. By reviewing recipe and procurement we identify between €150,000 and €500,000 per year in lower feedstock cost per plant.

What is Methan.IQ?

Methan.IQ is the platform operators use to record loads, deliveries and checks from a phone, while the owners receive a daily report on the plant. It is available as a monthly subscription per plant, including for plants that run their own operations.

Does FAR.E Group replace the plant team?

No. We work with the owners and the plant team: the field follows defined procedures, the owners and the asset manager decide based on the numbers.

Since when has FAR.E Group been operating?

FAR.E Group has been operating since 2023.
