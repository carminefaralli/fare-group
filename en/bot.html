Our automated reading systems

If you want to learn more or evaluate a collaboration, we are here.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What they do

We use automated systems that read public web pages to gather information about biogas and biomethane plants and the companies that operate them: technical data about the plants, company data, and general company contact details.

We read public pages. We do not access restricted areas, we do not bypass authentication, and we do not attempt to defeat protection measures.

How to recognise us

Our requests identify themselves in the user agent field as FAR.E Sales Office, together with the address of this page, so that anyone running a website can recognise us and, if they wish, get in touch.

How we behave

We follow the instructions in the site's robots.txt file.

We keep request rates low, so as not to affect the operation of the site.

We read a page when we need it, not at regular intervals and not systematically across an entire site.

We do not collect personal mobile numbers or personal email addresses. Our systems do not look for them and our records have no fields to store them.

We do not republish the content we read. The information gathered stays internal and is not an information product.

If you do not want to be read

Write to carmine.faralli@fare-group.com naming the domain concerned. We stop reading it and record the exclusion, without asking you for reasons.

You can achieve the same result through your site's robots.txt file.

Personal data

Where reading public pages leads us to gather data relating to an individual, that data is strictly professional and is covered by our privacy notice on commercial activity, which also explains how to object.
