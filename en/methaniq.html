Where it comes from

Born in the feedstock yard
of a real plant.

At a biogas plant we ran with our own operators on site, every load had to be traced and every feedstock had to be the one in the recipe. Spreadsheets were not enough.

That is how Methan.IQ was born. We built it for our own work, and today we also offer it to plants that run their own operations.

What it does

Everything that happens
at the plant, in order.

Features already in daily use in production, built around real plant problems.

Guided loading

The loading plan comes in from a file, feedstock by feedstock. The operator records from a phone how much has been loaded. Loading more than planned is not lost: the surplus becomes a credit for the next day. At the end of the day the figures are closed and can no longer be changed.

Feedstock certifiability

At delivery, the app checks that the feedstock is approved and included in the plan. If it is not, it asks for explicit confirmation and records it.

Deliveries against forecast

Week by week and feedstock by feedstock, how much arrived in the storage bunker compared with what was expected.

Stock levels

Always up-to-date stock, with an alert when a feedstock will not cover the next 7 days.

Daily email report

Every morning the owners receive a summary of the previous day: production, loaded against prescribed, deliveries against forecast and deadlines, with deviations highlighted.

Diagnostic simulator

Compares the theoretical production expected from the recipe with the measured one. The gap is not hidden: it is exactly the information that tells you where to look.

Plant performance

Energy produced and the trend of the last seven days, right on the app's home screen.

Sustainability report

Feeding and deliveries for the period in two Excel files, with a filter for approved feedstock only.

QR rounds

Plant rounds are recorded by scanning a QR code, straight from a phone.

Frequently asked questions

Methan.IQ at a glance

What is Methan.IQ?

Methan.IQ is a platform for the day-to-day operation of biogas and biomethane plants. Operators record loads, deliveries, checks and rounds from a phone; the owners receive a daily report with production, loaded against prescribed and deliveries against forecast.

Who develops Methan.IQ?

Methan.IQ is developed by FAR.E Services, a FAR.E Group company. It grew out of the work on a biogas plant run by FAR.E with its own operators on site.

Do I have to hand my plant over to FAR.E Group to use it?

No. Methan.IQ is also available to plants that run their own operations.

How much does Methan.IQ cost?

Methan.IQ is offered as a monthly subscription per plant, in two service levels. The price depends on the plant and is presented during the demo.
