For funds and investors in biogas and biomethane plants

Clear, verifiable plant numbers, before and after the investment. And the same indicators across every plant in the portfolio.

The problem with biogas is often not technology. It is management. Margin is lost in how feedstock is bought and how work is organised.

We are an independent technology operator: we do not own plants and we do not sell technology. We work only on the plant's results.

Clear, verifiable numbers

One indicator: what it costs to produce a cubic metre of methane

We measure the plant on the cost per cubic metre of methane. From there you can see which recipe pays, which contracts to review and where the plant loses margin. Loads, deliveries and production are recorded on the spot, not at the end of the month.

  • Cost per cubic metre of methane as the main indicator
  • A report every morning, without having to ask
  • Deviations from the plan highlighted
  • Unapproved feedstock flagged and traced

One method for the whole portfolio

The same procedures and indicators at every plant

For those with several plants we bring a method that is the same everywhere: written procedures, assigned responsibilities and comparable data. The asset manager governs with daily numbers; the platform works for the asset manager, not in their place.

  • Standard field procedures, the same for every shift
  • Indicators comparable across plants
  • Daily report to the owners and the asset manager
  • Weekly debrief and quarterly meeting in the Methan.IQ Full governance tier

Frequently asked questions

Funds and investors: frequently asked questions

Do you work with funds and investors?

Yes. We work with those who own, finance or develop biogas and biomethane plants: clear, verifiable plant numbers, before and after the investment.

How is a plant's performance measured?

On the cost per cubic metre of methane produced. This indicator shows which recipe pays, which contracts to review and where the plant loses margin.

Can you standardise the management of several plants?

Yes: the same procedures and the same indicators across every plant in the portfolio, with a daily report to the owners.

Is FAR.E Group independent?

Yes. It is an independent technology operator: it does not own plants and does not sell plants or machinery.
