About us

An Italian group that helps owners, funds and developers get more out of biogas and biomethane plants.

In short

What FAR.E Group is

FAR.E Group is an Italian group working on biogas and biomethane plants. We handle feedstock and recipe, plant operations on behalf of the owners, project management and technical and contract advisory, and we develop the Methan.IQ platform.

We have been operating since 2023, with our registered office in Isernia and offices in Florence. We work across Italy with a regional network: a national organisation, close to the area of every plant.

We always work together with the owners and the plant team: we bring method, tools and control of the numbers, while decisions stay with those who own the plant.

We are an independent technology operator: we do not own plants and we do not sell technology. We work only for the plant's results.

Where we come from

Industrial method, applied to biogas

Our technical core comes from Oil & Gas and large industrial groups. That is where we learned to work with written procedures, assigned responsibilities and lean methods.

Biogas started out as farming, but today it is an energy industry. Yet many plants are still run on instinct: feedstock is bought by the tonne, each shift works its own way, numbers arrive at month end.

We sell industrial processes, not consulting hours.

Procedures

Every field activity has a written procedure, the same for every shift.

Responsibilities

It is always clear who decides and who carries out. The owners stay at the centre of every choice.

Numbers

The cost of methane is measured, and data reaches the people who decide every day.
