Project management and technical advisory for biomethane plants

We follow the construction and start-up of plants and support the owners on technical contracts.

A plant runs well if it is set up well. Recipe, procurement and field organisation are decided before start-up, not after.

The largest plant we follow in project management has a capacity of 1,000 Sm³/h.

What it includes

Project management and technical and contract advisory

We support owners and developers from construction to start-up, with the same method we use at operating plants: procedures, clear responsibilities and numbers.

  • Coordination of suppliers and activities
  • Technical advisory on contracts
  • Recipe and procurement set up before start-up
  • Field work organised from day one

Why start before start-up

Set-up mistakes cost for years

Many plants lose margin without noticing: feedstock bought by the tonne, field work without procedures, numbers that arrive at the end of the month. Setting up recipe, contracts and procedures before start-up avoids having to fix them once the plant is running.

  • Recipe judged on the cost per cubic metre of methane from the start
  • Supply contracts and logistics already defined
  • Field procedures and responsibilities assigned from day one
  • Methan.IQ ready to trace loads and deliveries

Frequently asked questions

Project management: frequently asked questions

What do you cover in a plant's project management?

Coordination of suppliers and activities, technical advisory on contracts, setting up recipe and procurement before start-up and organising field work from day one.

What size of plants do you work on?

The largest plant we follow in project management has a capacity of 1,000 Sm³/h.

Why set up recipe and procurement before start-up?

Because the cost that matters is the cost of methane: choosing feedstock and contracts on the cost per cubic metre of methane from the start avoids buying by the tonne and having to fix the recipe once the plant is running.

Can you also run the plant after start-up?

Yes. FAR.E Energy runs plants on behalf of their owners, and the services also work together: feedstock, plant operations and Methan.IQ.

Are you developing a plant?

Tell us about the project: together we set up recipe, contracts and field organisation.
