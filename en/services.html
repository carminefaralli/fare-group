Services

What we can do for your biogas or biomethane plant. One service or all of them, always alongside the owners.

One shared goal: producing methane at a lower cost. Each service below works on part of that cost.

We work with the owners and the plant team: we bring method, tools and control of the numbers, while decisions stay with those who own the plant.

Feedstock and recipe

Feedstock management

We look at what goes into the digester, what it costs and how much methane it really produces. Then we redesign recipe and procurement to lower the cost per cubic metre of methane. By reviewing plant recipes and procurement, we identify between €150,000 and €500,000 per year in lower feedstock cost per plant.

  • Review of recipe and supply contracts
  • Recipe designed on the cost per cubic metre of methane
  • Supplier selection and incoming quality control
  • Logistics and traceability of deliveries
  • Today we follow 9 plants: 6 operating and 3 in development

Plant operations

Running the plant on behalf of the owners

We run the plant on behalf of those who own it, with standard procedures and clear responsibilities. We do not own plants: we work for the owners and report to them every day.

  • Operators on site working to standard procedures
  • Feedstock yard, loading and storage
  • Every load traced in Methan.IQ
  • Preventive maintenance with a quarterly plan
  • Critical spare parts in stock and emergency procedures
Read the case study

Projects

Project management and technical and contract advisory

We follow the construction and start-up of plants and support the owners on technical contracts. The largest plant we follow in project management has a capacity of 1,000 Sm³/h.

  • Coordination of suppliers and activities
  • Technical advisory on contracts
  • Recipe and procurement set up before start-up
  • Field organisation from day one

Methan.IQ

The platform for running the plant from a phone

It is the tool we use in the field, and we also offer it to plants that run their own operations, as a monthly subscription per plant. The field follows defined procedures, the owners receive a report every day.

  • Guided loading against a loading plan
  • Feedstock certifiability check
  • Deliveries against forecast and stock levels
  • Daily email report and sustainability report
  • Simulator: theoretical and measured production
Discover Methan.IQ

The services work on their own, but they give their best together: a better recipe is of little use if it is not followed in the field, and data only matters if it reaches those who decide.

Which service do we start from?

Tell us about your plant: we usually start from recipe and supply contracts, where savings are quickest to measure.
